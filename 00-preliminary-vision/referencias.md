## Marco metodológico

- **TOGAF® Standard, Version 9.2** — The Open Group. Architecture Development Method (ADM), fases Preliminary, Architecture Vision, Business Architecture e Information Systems Architecture.
- **ArchiMate® 3.2 Specification** — The Open Group. Motivation Elements: Stakeholder, Driver, Assessment, Goal, Outcome, Principle, Requirement, Constraint.
- **BPMN 2.0** — Object Management Group. Notación de modelado de procesos de negocio.
